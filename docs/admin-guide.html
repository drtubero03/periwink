01

Accessing the Admin Console

Go to periwink-rjmcuborrq-ue.a.run.app/admin in your browser.

You will be immediately prompted for a password. Enter your admin password and press OK. The password is stored in your browser tab for the session — if you close the tab and return, you'll be asked again.

Keep your password private. Anyone with it has full control over the community — user data, posts, applications, and moderation.

If you enter the wrong password, you'll see an "Access denied" message. Simply navigate back to /admin and try again.

02

Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It gives you a quick health check of the community at a glance:

  • Community Signups — people who joined your waitlist
  • Applications — founding member applications received
  • Pending Review — applications you haven't acted on yet (highlighted in amber when there are some)
  • Users — accounts fully created on the platform
  • Posts / Comments / Reactions — community engagement metrics
  • Symptom Logs — how many times members have used the tracker
  • Flags Pending — content flagged by the AI that needs your review (highlighted in red when urgent)

Below the stats you'll see a signup trend chart (last 30 days) and two panels showing your most recent signups and recent users.

Use the Refresh button (top right of the tab bar) any time to reload the latest data.

03

Community Activity

The Activity tab shows a live feed of all posts and comments across every room, in reverse chronological order.

Each card shows:

  • Whether it's a Post or a Reply
  • The room it was posted in
  • The author's display name (or "Anonymous" for anonymous posts)
  • A preview of the content
  • How long ago it was posted

Posts or comments marked Hidden will appear faded — they are hidden from regular members but still visible to you here.

AI-generated content (from the AI Guide bot, if enabled) is marked with a purple AI Guide badge and a slightly different background.

04

Moderation Flags

The Flags tab is your moderation queue. When the AI detects content that may violate community guidelines, it creates a flag for your review.

Flags have three severity levels:

  • HIGH Potentially harmful — review promptly
  • MEDIUM Borderline content — use your judgement
  • LOW Minor concern — informational

For each flag you'll see the content, the AI's reason for flagging it, and two action buttons:

  • Mark Reviewed — you've seen it and taken action (e.g., the post is already hidden)
  • Dismiss — the flag was a false positive, no action needed

To hide a flagged post, go to the Posts tab, find the post, and click Hide. Then come back to Flags and mark it Reviewed.

The tab defaults to showing only PENDING flags. Use the filter buttons to view Reviewed or Dismissed flags too.

05

Signups

The Signups tab lists everyone who submitted their email through your landing page waitlist form. These people are interested in joining but have not yet created an account.

Each entry shows their name, email address, chosen pseudonym, and the date they signed up. You can click any email address to open a draft email to them directly.

Use the search bar to filter by name, email, or pseudonym.

06

Founding Member Applications

The Applications tab manages people who applied to be founding members. This is a more curated group than general signups.

Each application shows the person's name, email, the role type they applied for, and the current status:

  • PENDING New, not yet reviewed
  • REVIEWING You're actively considering this person
  • APPROVED Accepted as a founding member
  • DECLINED Not accepted

To view full application details, click anywhere on a row — it expands to show what drew them to Periwink, what they offer, their organization, and website.

To update status, use the dropdown at the end of the row. Changes save immediately.

Use the filter buttons at the top to see only Pending, Reviewing, Approved, or Declined applications.

07

Users

The Users tab lists everyone who has created a full account on Periwink. Unlike signups, these people can log in and participate in the community.

Each row shows their display name, email, menopause stage (if they shared it during onboarding), and when they joined.

Use the search bar to find someone by email or display name. You can click their email to draft a message to them.

08

Posts

The Posts tab gives you full moderation control over community content.

For each post you can:

  • Pin / Unpin — pinned posts appear at the top of the room
  • Hide / Show — hides a post from regular members without deleting it
  • Lock / Unlock — prevents new comments on a post
  • Delete — soft-deletes the post (it's not shown but remains in the database)

Deletion is permanent from the user's perspective. The post disappears from the community immediately. Use Hide first if you're unsure.

Use the search bar to find posts by title, content, author name, or room name. Anonymous posts show "Anonymous" as the author.

09

Rooms

The Rooms tab shows all the discussion rooms on the platform — their name, emoji icon, how many posts and followers each has, whether they're a default room (shown to all new members), and their display order.

At the bottom of the tab, you can create a new room by filling in:

  • Room name — e.g., "Hot Flashes"
  • Slug — the URL-friendly version, e.g., hot-flashes (no spaces, lowercase)
  • Description — one sentence about what the room is for
  • Icon — an emoji, e.g., 🔥
  • Sort order — lower numbers appear first in the list
  • Default room — check this if new members should automatically follow this room
10

Moderators

The Moderators tab lets you assign trusted community members as moderators of specific rooms.

Moderators have two roles:

  • Moderator — can help manage content in their assigned room
  • Owner — full ownership of the room

To add a moderator, select a user from the dropdown, choose their room, set their role, and click Add. To remove one, click the Remove button next to their row.

Only users who have already created accounts will appear in the user dropdown. If someone isn't showing up, they may not have signed up yet.
